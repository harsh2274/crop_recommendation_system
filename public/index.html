What matters most

    How a farmer uses it: by text message

    No phone needed to try it: open the SMS simulator. It is the same assistant, map, analysis and PDF report, with the texts shown in your browser.

    1. The farmer texts the service number in their language, for example "Hola, cultivo maíz".
    2. They get a link to a one-page map: Find my farm → Start drawing → Continue.
    3. A few simple questions follow: past crops, crop stage, problems, water, what matters most. Numbers work: "3".
    4. A summary to confirm, then YES.
    5. About a minute later, 4 plain texts arrive: their crops, the suggested plan, things to watch, an advisor note.
    6. Any time after that: "How and where should I sell my crops?", "why sunflower?", LINK to redraw, NEW, RESET.

    Facts come from code; the AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI) only reads replies into fields and translates. A translation that changes any number is rejected and the English original is sent instead.

    What's inside

    NASA data

    • POWER: 20 years of daily temperature, rain, humidity, solar radiation, wind, root-zone wetness
    • MODIS NDVI: greenness, bare months, observed green-up
    • SRTM: slope and wet low spots
    • EONET: fires, floods, storms nearby

    Plus

    • NOAA weather-station correction
    • USDA soil survey (SSURGO) / SoilGrids
    • USDA crop maps: what grew on the field
    • IMF prices and USDA supply/demand for the market answer

    Methods

    • 18 crops replayed through 20 real seasons with a daily soil-water model
    • Variety length fitted to the local season
    • Rotation plans scored for soil health, water, resilience, pests, erosion; a ±20% robustness test
    • Backtested harvest-price ranges

    Runs entirely on Cloudflare: Workers, Agents SDK (Durable Objects), Workers AI, KV, D1 and Cron Triggers. Decision support only: it does not replace farmer judgment or local agronomic advice.
